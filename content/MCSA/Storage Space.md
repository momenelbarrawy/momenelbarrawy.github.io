# Storage Spaces Resiliency Types

## Automatic

- Automatically repairs failed drives.
- Data is rebuilt without administrator intervention.
- Recommended for production environments.
- Requires available free capacity or a hot spare.

---

## Hot Spare

- A standby disk kept unused until another disk fails.
- Automatically replaces the failed drive.
- Rebuild starts immediately after failure.
- Improves availability and reduces downtime.

---

## Manual

- Administrator must replace the failed drive.
- Repair/rebuild is started manually.
- No automatic recovery.
- Suitable for labs or small environments.
